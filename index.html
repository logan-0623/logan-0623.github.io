Research Focus

ONGOING · CMU

How do pretrained vision-language models encode space and physics, and does that knowledge reach the actions a robot takes? My work follows one loop:

  1. 01Observe→

    Scenes, language, proprioception

    Camera views, an instruction, and the robot state. In LIBERO I build counterfactual scene pairs that differ in one geometric factor.

  2. 02Represent→

    VLM / VLA features

    Pretrained VLMs such as Qwen3-VL produce visual-language features. The open question: do they encode spatial and physical structure, and does it survive into a VLA policy? Measured with representation probes.

  3. 03Act→

    Action generation

    A VLA maps those features to robot actions. In GVLA the policy is also conditioned on gripper morphology, so one model can adapt its strategy to the end-effector.

  4. 04Validate

    Causal and real-world tests

    Activation interventions test whether a representation actually changes the action; rollouts in simulation and on Franka / UR5 measure task success.

The CMU work is in progress: these are the questions and methods, not yet findings.
