Groundwork for an autonomous magnetic capsule endoscopy arm at Jifu Medical. Led the teleoperation and data collection pipeline on SO-101 dual-arm robots with LeRobot (ACT / ALOHA): synchronized multi-view recording and joint-state / action logging, with 50+ episodes recorded and validated. 05 / 2025

Self-Balancing Wheeled-Legged Robot

Independent Build

Built end-to-end on the open-source StackForce bipedal wheeled platform: an ESP32-S3 with an MPU6050 IMU, two BLDC hub motors with magnetic encoders, and four servos driving five-bar legs. Firmware in embedded C++: five-bar inverse kinematics, a cascaded PID balance loop (speed → pitch → wheel torque), yaw-rate control, and roll-based leg-height adaptation for rough terrain, under SBUS remote control.
