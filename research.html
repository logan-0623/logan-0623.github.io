04 / MEDICAL ROBOTICS

Autonomous magnetic capsule endoscopy

Jifu Medical makes magnetically controlled capsule endoscopy. The patient swallows a capsule camera, and an external magnet on a robotic arm steers it through the stomach and intestine. A full examination needs no anaesthesia, which matters for elderly patients who cannot be sedated, and it spares patients the discomfort of a conventional scope.

The arm was driven by hand by a doctor. I joined to help build the first VLA-style autonomous version: perceive the patient and the capsule's view, judge whether the whole stomach has been covered, and move the magnet to steer the capsule on its own.
